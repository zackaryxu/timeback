The Hideout

Belmont Parks & Recreation

September 15, 2026

What each piece represents

At the Hideout, Belmont’s after-school program for middle schoolers, TimeBack ran the jar activity with three kinds of pieces and asked what each one stands for in a week. The group then worked through ways to catch yourself procrastinating, and closed on the idea that your 24 hours are yours to make count.

Led by Zackary Xu, Brandon Axelson, Daniel Szeto, Logan Tan.
