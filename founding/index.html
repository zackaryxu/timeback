What is a TimeBack chapter?

A TimeBack chapter brings students together to learn and practice time management. Chapter Leads have the freedom to shape meetings, workshops, and activities around their school or community, with curriculum and digital materials provided by TimeBack. Chapters can start small and develop at their own pace.
