Work with TimeBack

Who we work with

We partner with places where young people learn and connect, including:

  • Schools

    Classrooms, advisory periods and student clubs.

  • Libraries

    Homework centers, teen programs and drop-in spaces.

  • Community groups

    Youth programs, enrichment groups and more.

What TimeBack brings

Free workshops

  • We bring all the materials.
  • Our facilitators come to you and lead the session, usually 30–45 minutes.
  • We adapt each lesson to your middle and high school students’ ages, interests and setting.

What students gain

  • Goal setting and prioritization to make time for what matters.
  • Practical ways to plan their time and handle distractions.
  • Hands-on activities and discussion with peers.
  • A chance to ask older students about school, workload and balancing their interests.
Explore our approach

What we appreciate

  • Spreading the word. Mentioning TimeBack in your workshop promotion to help students hear about it.
  • Knowing your group. Sharing students’ ages and interests so we can design the lesson around them.
  • Timing. For 2026, we’re prioritizing workshops earlier in the fall so students have time to practice these skills before exams. We’re flexible on dates, with lighter availability in December as school workloads increase.

Develop your student leaders

We train your students to lead TimeBack workshops themselves.

  • Lesson plans
  • Facilitator training
  • Coaching along the way
