TimeBack

Free time management workshops, taught by students.

Who we are

I'm Zackary Xu, a student at Carlmont High School in Belmont, California. I started TimeBack and run it. My friend Brandon Axelson helps me put on the sessions.

How it started

In high school you notice it fast. Some people are always on top of everything, and everyone else is drowning, and it never lines up with how smart anyone is.

I was building software for students at the time, so I went looking into why. It kept coming back to habits, the things that most people weren't taught (or even aware of).

So I decided to teach it. I pitched the idea at Carlmont's YECB Community Impact Pitch Competition, won the top individual prize, and used it to start running sessions at school and around our community.

What we do

Time management isn't really about time. It's about getting to the things you actually care about, and having fun while you do it.

So we teach the parts underneath. Prioritizing toward a goal, strategies for focus, cutting the distractions that pull you off it, and making your fun count instead of leaking away. Each session takes on a different piece.

Sessions run 30 to 45 minutes, hands-on rather than a lecture, and we adjust them depending on the group.

Our plans

We're bringing these to teen programs, libraries, and schools around San Mateo County this year. Every session is free and we bring all the materials.
