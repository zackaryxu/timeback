TimeBack Organization

Learning to manage time should be part of growing up.

Time management
is a life skill.

TimeBack is a youth-led education organization dedicated to helping young people align their time with their goals in a world of distraction.

Through free workshops, peer learning and shared curriculum, middle and high school students practice setting priorities, managing distractions and building habits that help them follow through on their responsibilities.

Connecting young people through time-management education. Students share what they learn in their schools and communities, helping one another take ownership of their time.

Learning that goes both ways.

For students

Our free workshops teach practical time-management skills through activities and guidance from older peers.

  • Make time for what matters. Connect everyday choices to your goals.
  • Build practical habits. Try ways to plan your time and handle distractions.
  • Learn alongside others. Exchange ideas through activities and discussion.
  • Ask older peers. Talk through school, workload and balancing your interests.
Work with TimeBack

For workshop leaders

Build your skills as a teacher and leader. TimeBack provides curriculum and coaching to help you get started.

  • Learn more deeply by teaching. Preparing and explaining ideas can strengthen your own understanding. Research on learning by teaching
  • Lead with responsibility. Plan sessions, organize a team and follow through.
  • Work with other leaders. Share ideas, plan together and learn from fellow facilitators.
  • Make learning engaging. Practice public speaking, guide discussion and design activities for your peers.
