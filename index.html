Your time.Your questions.

A free workshop where students teach students how to manage their time, plan their week, and beat procrastination.

What a session looks like

  1. The jar. A demo about why some weeks fit and some fall apart. It takes about a minute to land and nobody forgets it.
  2. Your own week. Everyone writes down what actually matters in theirs, and takes it home.
  3. Ask us anything. Open questions about high school, answered by two people currently in it.

It runs 30 to 45 minutes, it's completely free, we bring all the materials, and we adjust it depending on the group.

Why we started it

A lot of our classmates, even the ones in Honors and AP, lose hours every week and never get taught how not to. Going from middle school to Carlmont, the workload jumped more than we expected, and we ended up building our own systems the hard way. This is the version we wish someone had just shown us.

TimeBack is run by Zackary Xu and Brandon Axelson, students at Carlmont High School in Belmont, California. We've held sessions at Carlmont and around San Mateo County, and we're always looking for the next place to run one.
