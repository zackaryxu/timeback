TimeBack

Free time management workshops, taught by students.

Who we are

I'm Zackary Xu, a student at Carlmont High School in Belmont, California. I started TimeBack and run it. My friend Brandon Axelson helps me put on the sessions.

How it started

I'd been building a productivity app, and kept running into the same thing. People don't just need better tools. They need better habits, and nobody really teaches that part.

So we started teaching it ourselves, to the people around us who needed it, which turned out to be almost everyone.

What we do

A session runs 30 to 45 minutes. Students figure out where their time actually goes, sort out what matters in their week, and leave with one thing they're going to change. It's hands-on, not a lecture, and we adjust it depending on the group.

Our plans

We're bringing these to teen programs, libraries, and schools around San Mateo County this year. Every session is free and we bring all the materials.
