How TimeBack began

TimeBack began with the idea that young people should have opportunities to learn how to manage their time, through practice and conversations with peers.

Zackary founded TimeBack at Carlmont High School in 2026 and assembled a team to bring its workshops into schools and communities.

In 2026, Zackary received a prize in Carlmont’s Youth Engagement Capacity Building pitch competition. Scot Scoop’s coverage of the competition
